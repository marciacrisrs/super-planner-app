# UX 10 — Product Decisions

## Decision 001 — Neutral audience, informed by the original audience

The product is designed for a broad audience. The original inspiration is women 30+ dealing with multiple roles and competing priorities, but the interface must not presume gender or a particular life configuration.

## Decision 002 — The product suggests, the person decides

The core phrase is not “the planner tells you what to do”. The product **helps you decide what to do now**, based on the person's own goals, priorities and current reality.

## Decision 003 — “Agora” is the primary value moment

The most important UX outcome is not a perfectly filled calendar. It is helping a person make the next useful decision with low cognitive effort.

## Decision 004 — Recommendations must preserve agency

Recommendations are presented as options. Consequential changes require explicit user action. The product must never imply that the AI owns the person's priorities.

## Decision 005 — Usability outranks visual polish

When a visual treatment conflicts with clarity, speed, accessibility or user control, usability wins.

## Decision 006 — No planner administration tax

New features must not require the person to continuously maintain metadata, dashboards or configuration merely to receive useful planning help.
