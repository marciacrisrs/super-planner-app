# UX 10 — Wave 1 completion

## Direction

The Super Planner helps the person decide what to do now according to their goals, priorities and current reality. It suggests; the person decides.

## Wave 1 acceptance

- Primary product language uses autonomy-first wording.
- The primary value moment is Agora.
- Recommendation copy explains a causal reason without implying authority.
- Visual hierarchy prioritizes the decision, not planner administration.
- Empty and completed states avoid guilt language.
- Accessibility is treated as a product requirement, not a polish pass.

## Next gate

Wave 2 is complete only when a person can answer, at a glance:

1. What can I do now?
2. How long do I have?
3. What is coming next?
4. Why is this a suggestion?
5. What else could I choose?

If the interface cannot answer those questions quickly, improve the experience before adding another planning feature.
